About

I build firmware for electric vehicles and their chargers: the charging communication stack for Ather Energy’s LECCS standard, rectifier control for DC fast chargers, FreeRTOS software for battery management systems, and CAN software for anti-lock braking.

I’m now specialising in automotive embedded security: UDS bootloaders, secure boot and authenticated CAN communication. My projects below are where I build and test that work in public.
